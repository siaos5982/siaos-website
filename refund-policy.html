1. Requesting a return or refund

Contact SIAOS through the Contact page with your name, order or booking reference, transaction date, reason and relevant photographs or documents. For a physical product, make the request within 7 days after recorded delivery. A request does not automatically approve a return; wait for return instructions before sending an item.

2. Eligible physical-product returns

An item may be eligible when it is unused, unworn, unaltered and returned with its original packaging, accessories and proof of purchase. After inspection, SIAOS may offer a replacement, store resolution or refund according to the circumstances and your applicable rights.

For a discretionary change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for safe return shipping. Original delivery charges and actual return costs may be non-refundable where permitted by law. These costs are not charged when SIAOS supplied an incorrect, defective or materially damaged item.

3. Items that may not be returnable

For hygiene, safety or personalisation reasons, an opened fragrance, cosmetic or body-use product; a used or worn item; a personalised, energised or customer-specific item; and an item damaged after delivery may be ineligible for a change-of-mind return. This exclusion does not remove remedies for an item that was defective, damaged, incorrect or materially different when supplied.

4. Damaged, tampered, missing or incorrect delivery

Notify SIAOS within 48 hours after delivery when the parcel is visibly damaged, tampered, incomplete or incorrect. Keep the shipping label, external packaging and product. Clear photographs and an unedited package-opening video, where available, help us investigate quickly. SIAOS may verify the claim with the courier before approving replacement or refund.

5. Consultations and digital reports

Consultation refunds follow the Cancellation Policy. If SIAOS cannot provide a confirmed consultation and you do not accept a replacement time, the consultation amount will be refunded.

A personalised or accessed digital report is generally non-returnable. A refund or corrected delivery may be offered for duplicate payment, non-delivery, a materially incomplete file, persistent technical failure or where applicable law requires it. Interpretive disagreement with astrology, tarot, numerology or Vastu guidance is not by itself a defect or guarantee of a refund.

6. Failed, pending and duplicate payments

If money is debited but no order or booking is confirmed, first allow the payment provider and bank time to reverse or reconcile the transaction. Contact SIAOS with the gateway reference if the status remains unresolved. A verified duplicate payment will be refunded to the original method.

7. Inspection and refund timing

For a returned physical product, SIAOS will inspect the item after receipt. If approved, the refund will be initiated to the original payment method. SIAOS aims to initiate an approved refund within 7 business days; the bank or payment provider may require additional time to display it.

Nothing in this policy limits a remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Indian consumer law.
