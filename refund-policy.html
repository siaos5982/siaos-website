1. How to report an eligible issue

Contact SIAOS through the Contact page with your name, order or booking reference, transaction date, reason and relevant evidence. Do not send a product back without written return instructions from SIAOS.

2. Physical products are final sale

SIAOS offers no voluntary return, refund, replacement, exchange or cancellation on a physical product after payment confirmation.

This includes change of mind, incorrect selection by the customer, dislike of reasonable colour, texture, size, appearance or aroma variation, and a product no longer being wanted. Natural stones, fragrances, handcrafted items and ritual products may reasonably vary from photographs.

3. Wrong, defective, damaged or misdescribed products

The final-sale rule does not exclude remedies that cannot lawfully be waived. Notify SIAOS promptly if the item supplied is wrong, defective, damaged, spurious, incomplete or materially different from its description. Preserve the shipping label, external packaging and product, and provide clear photographs plus an unedited package-opening video where available.

After verification, SIAOS will provide the remedy required by applicable Indian consumer law. As the business does not operate a voluntary replacement programme, an approved product remedy will ordinarily be processed as a return and refund rather than a replacement, unless the law or an agreed resolution requires otherwise.

4. Consultation cancellation refunds

Consultation cancellations receive 75% when acknowledged 20 or more hours before the appointment, 50% from 12 to under 20 hours, 25% from 5 to under 12 hours, and 0% under 5 hours or for a no-show. Full details are in the Cancellation Policy. If SIAOS cancels and you decline a replacement time, the consultation receives a 100% refund.

5. Digital reports

A personalised or accessed digital report is generally non-refundable. A refund or corrected delivery may be offered for duplicate payment, non-delivery, a materially incomplete file, persistent technical failure or where applicable law requires it. Interpretive disagreement with astrology, tarot, numerology or Vastu guidance is not by itself a defect or guarantee of a refund.

6. Failed, pending and duplicate payments

Website Razorpay payments apply only to products and the ₹99 compatibility report. Consultation payments are arranged in the verified WhatsApp conversation. If money is debited but no order or appointment is confirmed, contact SIAOS with the transaction reference. A verified duplicate payment will be refunded to the original method where supported.

7. Refund processing

Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method where supported, or through another method agreed with the customer for a directly paid consultation. SIAOS aims to initiate an approved refund within 7 business days; the bank or payment provider may require additional time to display it. For a legally eligible physical-product issue, inspection may be required after the item is returned using SIAOS instructions.

Nothing in this policy limits a remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Indian consumer law.
