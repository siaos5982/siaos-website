The month’s transit backdrop

Dates used: 15 May 2026.

Verified frame: Surya enters Vrishabha on 15 May. Drik Panchang lists Shani's nakshatra movement into Revati on 17 May; this is a nakshatra change, not a Rashi ingress.

How to read this: The mini-briefs below use the transit through the relevant house from each Moon sign/Rashi. A natal chart, ascendant, dasha and exact location can materially change an astrologer's reading.

Mesha (Aries) — transit emphasis: house 2

Career and work: Explore income structure, skill value and dependable output; treat this as a planning lens, not an event promise.

Money: review cash flow, subscriptions and the price placed on your work.

Relationships: discuss values and shared resources calmly.

Wellbeing: favour regular meals, rest and sustainable pace.

The interval to watch: Watch for possessiveness or measuring self-worth through money.

Your practical next scene: List three resources already available and assign each a practical use.

Vrishabha (Taurus) — transit emphasis: house 1

Career and work: Explore initiative, role definition and visible ownership; treat this as a planning lens, not an event promise.

Money: set a spending ceiling before beginning something new.

Relationships: state needs directly without turning independence into distance.

Wellbeing: protect sleep and recovery while momentum rises.

The interval to watch: Watch for impulsive starts or confusing urgency with importance.

Your practical next scene: Choose one priority, define the first measurable step, and delay nonessential commitments for 24 hours.

Wellbeing: prioritise sleep, reflection and professional help when distress persists.

The interval to watch: Watch for avoidance, isolation or making decisions while emotionally flooded.

Karka (Cancer) — transit emphasis: house 11

Relationships: choose reciprocal communities rather than chasing approval.

Wellbeing: balance online connection with embodied routines.

The interval to watch: Watch for overcommitting to causes or relying on vague future gains.
