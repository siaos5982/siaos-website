1. How to request a cancellation

Contact SIAOS through the verified channels on the Contact page. Provide the customer name, order or booking reference, transaction date and the item or appointment to be cancelled. A request is effective when acknowledged by SIAOS, not merely when a message is sent.

2. Physical-product orders

A physical-product order may be cancelled for a full refund before dispatch. Once the courier booking or dispatch process has begun, cancellation may no longer be possible. You may instead request a return or resolution under the Refund Policy.

If an order is refused at delivery or returned because of an incorrect address or unavailable recipient, actual outward and return shipping costs may be deducted where permitted by law, unless SIAOS or the courier caused the failure.

3. Consultation bookings

You may cancel or request one rescheduling at least 24 hours before the confirmed appointment time. A timely cancellation is eligible for a full refund. A timely rescheduling request is subject to available slots.

For a request received less than 24 hours before the appointment, SIAOS will reasonably consider rescheduling, but a refund is not guaranteed because the professional time was reserved. If you do not attend and provide no notice, the booking may be treated as completed for scheduling purposes.

If SIAOS needs to change or cancel a confirmed appointment, you may choose an available replacement slot or a full refund for that consultation.

4. Digital reports and immediately supplied content

A digital report order can be cancelled before generation, personalisation or access is provided. Once a personalised report has been generated or digital access has been delivered or used, cancellation is generally unavailable, except for duplicate payment, non-delivery, material technical failure or rights available under applicable law.

5. Cancellation by SIAOS

SIAOS may cancel an order or booking when payment cannot be verified, information required for fulfilment is missing, an item is unavailable, a catalogue or price error occurred, service cannot safely or lawfully be provided, abusive or fraudulent activity is detected, or an event outside reasonable control prevents fulfilment. Where SIAOS cancels after successful payment and no service or product has been supplied, the relevant amount will be refunded.

6. Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank and payment-provider processing time applies after SIAOS initiates the refund. See the Refund Policy for expected timing and failed or duplicate payment handling.
