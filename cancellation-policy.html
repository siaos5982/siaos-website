1. How to request a cancellation

For a consultation, reply in the same verified WhatsApp conversation used to confirm the appointment. For other requests, use the verified channels on the Contact page. Provide the customer name, order or booking reference, transaction date and the item or appointment to be cancelled. A request is effective when acknowledged by SIAOS, not merely when a message is sent.

2. Physical-product orders

All physical-product orders are final sale once payment is confirmed. SIAOS does not accept voluntary cancellation, return, replacement, exchange or refund because of change of mind, an incorrectly selected product or variant, dislike of reasonable natural variation, or the item no longer being required.

This final-sale rule does not remove remedies that must be provided under applicable Indian consumer law when an item is wrong, defective, damaged, spurious or materially different from its description, or when SIAOS cannot fulfil the order. See the Refund Policy.

3. Consultation bookings

The refund percentage is determined by the time between SIAOS acknowledging the cancellation request and the confirmed appointment start time.

75%20 hours or morebefore the appointment
50%12 to under 20 hoursbefore the appointment
25%5 to under 12 hoursbefore the appointment
0%Under 5 hoursor a no-show

Any rescheduling is subject to availability and written confirmation by SIAOS. A rescheduling request does not pause or replace this cancellation schedule unless SIAOS confirms the new appointment.

If SIAOS cancels a confirmed appointment, you may choose an available replacement slot or a 100% refund for that consultation.

4. Digital reports and immediately supplied content

A digital report order can be cancelled before generation, personalisation or access is provided. Once a personalised report has been generated or digital access has been delivered or used, cancellation is generally unavailable, except for duplicate payment, non-delivery, material technical failure or rights available under applicable law.

5. Cancellation by SIAOS

SIAOS may cancel an order or booking when payment cannot be verified, information required for fulfilment is missing, an item is unavailable, a catalogue or price error occurred, service cannot safely or lawfully be provided, abusive or fraudulent activity is detected, or an event outside reasonable control prevents fulfilment. Where SIAOS cancels after successful payment and no service or product has been supplied, the relevant amount will be refunded.

6. Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where supported, or through another method agreed with the customer when the consultation was paid directly. Bank and payment-provider processing time applies after SIAOS initiates the refund. See the Refund Policy for expected timing and failed or duplicate payment handling.
