Shipping, explained clearly.

This policy applies to physical products purchased from SIAOS. Consultations and digital reports are delivered electronically or through the confirmed consultation channel and are not shipped.

1. Delivery coverage

SIAOS currently accepts website delivery addresses within India. Serviceability depends on the courier network and postal code. If an accepted address is later found to be unserviceable, SIAOS will contact you to arrange an alternative or cancel and refund the affected order.

2. Processing and estimated delivery

The estimated delivery window displayed on each product page is the best estimate for that item. Current catalogue estimates generally range from 3–8 business days, depending on the product and destination. The estimate begins after payment confirmation and excludes Sundays, public holidays, address-verification delays and exceptional courier disruption.

Orders containing multiple products may arrive separately. An estimate is not a guaranteed delivery date, but SIAOS will provide reasonable support if a shipment is materially delayed.

3. Shipping charges

Any shipping charge will be shown in the order summary before payment. SIAOS will not add an undisclosed shipping charge after checkout. Government taxes or statutory charges, where applicable, are reflected as required in the transaction documentation.

4. Delivery address and changes

You are responsible for entering a complete name, reachable telephone number, address, locality, city, state and postal code. Contact SIAOS immediately if correction is required. An address change can be accepted only before dispatch and may not be possible after a shipping label or courier booking has been created.

5. Dispatch and tracking

When tracking is available, it may be shared by telephone, WhatsApp or the customer account. Tracking events are supplied by the courier and can take time to update after dispatch.

6. Delivery attempts and returned shipments

The courier may contact the telephone number supplied with the order. If delivery fails because the address is incomplete, the recipient is unavailable, delivery is refused, or required contact cannot be made, the parcel may return to SIAOS. Re-shipping may require payment of the actual additional shipping cost unless the failure was caused by SIAOS or the courier.

7. Delayed, lost, damaged or incorrect delivery

For a shipment that appears lost or materially delayed, contact SIAOS with the order reference so a courier investigation can be opened. For a damaged, tampered or incorrect package, preserve the packaging and contact SIAOS promptly with clear photographs and, where available, an unedited opening video. See the Refund Policy for resolution options.
