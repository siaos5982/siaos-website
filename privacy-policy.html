1. Scope and responsibility

This Privacy Policy applies to Sarkar Institute of Astrology and Occult Sciences (SIAOS), its website at siaos.in, customer accounts, consultation bookings, digital reports and product orders. By using a specific service, you provide the information necessary to deliver that service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, telephone number, email address if provided, account identifier and verification status.
  • Consultation information: selected service, appointment details and information you submit for the requested reading, which may include date, time and place of birth, relationship or family context, questions, photographs, plans or documents.
  • Order and delivery information: product, quantity, delivery name, telephone number and postal address.
  • Transaction records: amount, currency, payment status and gateway-generated order or payment references.
  • Communications: messages and supporting information you send when requesting support or making a complaint.
  • Consented website activity: anonymous visitor and session identifiers, page path without its query string, event name, device category and referring domain.

Please provide only information relevant to the service. Do not send passwords, OTPs, authentication tokens, CVVs or complete payment-card numbers.

3. How we use information

We use information to verify accounts; reserve and manage appointments; prepare and provide requested services; process orders and payments; deliver products or digital access; prevent fraud and abuse; provide support; maintain business, tax and payment records; improve the website with consented analytics; and meet legal obligations.

Consultation information is used to provide the requested guidance and related customer support. It is not used to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be handled by carefully selected providers that support SIAOS operations, such as Supabase for accounts and database services, Cloudflare for website and API security, Razorpay for payment processing, and Google Sheets for restricted operational reporting. Delivery details may be shared with a courier or logistics provider when an order is dispatched.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect customers and the service, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganisation subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

5. Payments

Payments are processed through Razorpay or another displayed payment provider. SIAOS receives payment references, status and transaction information required for fulfilment and reconciliation. SIAOS does not intentionally store your complete card number or CVV on its website. The payment provider’s own terms and privacy notice also apply.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The website uses browser storage needed for sign-in state, booking continuity, cart or checkout continuity and service preferences. Optional analytics operate only after consent and are designed not to collect form contents, OTPs, passwords, complete URLs containing query strings or complete referring URLs. You can decline optional analytics through the consent choice presented on the website and can clear browser storage through your browser settings.

7. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably required for the service, customer support, security, dispute resolution and applicable accounting, tax or legal requirements. Digital report access may expire after the period shown at purchase, while a limited purchase record may be retained. Anonymous analytics records are kept only for legitimate reporting and security needs.

8. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask SIAOS to confirm whether we hold your information, correct inaccurate information, delete information that is no longer required, withdraw an optional consent, or address a privacy grievance. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already completed and may make a requested service impossible to provide.

For account or privacy requests, contact the SIAOS Customer Support & Grievance Desk. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Security and confidentiality

SIAOS uses access controls, authenticated systems and restricted operational reporting designed to protect customer information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your phone and account session, use only official SIAOS links and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

Children

The website is not directed at children for independent account creation or purchasing. A parent or lawful guardian should make bookings or purchases involving a child and provide only necessary information.

10. Contact and updates

Privacy questions and grievances can be submitted through the verified channels on our Contact page. We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
